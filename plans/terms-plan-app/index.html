How to run this

01

A term you can name is a term you can learn. Half of feeling lost in a field is just unfamiliar words for familiar ideas.

02

Intuition over precision. Aim for the one-breath version first (a gradient is a slope; a token is a chunk of text). Depth comes from the other books.

03

Use it as a lookup, not a novel. The real win is opening it whenever a term stops you in another track, not memorizing it end to end.

04

Explain it back. You only truly know a term when you can define it in your own words to someone else — do that, don't just re-read.

05

Follow the sparks. When a definition makes you think 'wait, how does that actually work?', that's your cue to open the cookbook it points to.

06

Rhythm: ~1 hr/day × 4 days — quick and low-stakes. This is the easiest track in the library; treat it as the connective tissue for the rest.
