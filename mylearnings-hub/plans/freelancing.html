What you should already know — shore up any gaps first:

  • A sellable, in-demand skill (the rest of this library gives you a strong one)

  • Some tolerance for income variability while you ramp up

How to run this

01

Sell outcomes, not hours. Position around the client's problem. A specialist gets chosen and paid; a generalist competes with the planet on price.

02

Niche down — it's the highest-leverage move. And your AI/ML skills (RAG, agents, fine-tuning) are a rare, premium niche. Lead with them.

03

Proposals win on relevance, not volume. Read the post, lead with a first line specific to it, apply early, bid selectively.

04

Price with confidence. Underpricing repels good clients and traps you at the bottom. Charge for value; raise as your reviews grow.

05

Communicate relentlessly and scope in writing. Going silent and absorbing scope creep are the two biggest killers of freelance projects.

06

Reputation compounds. Optimize the first jobs for reviews and repeat clients, then climb the leverage ladder into a real business.

07

Rhythm: ~2 hrs/day × 4 days — mostly doing (profile, proposals, outreach), not just reading. This is a sales skill; you learn it by shipping bids.
